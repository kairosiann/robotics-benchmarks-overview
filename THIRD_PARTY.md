# Third-party sources and reuse status

This catalog cites and preserves official benchmark task definitions, checker excerpts, papers, webpages, and media. The benchmark authors retain rights in their material. The catalog's code, the source snapshots, the JSON excerpts, and the reproduced figures do not share one automatically inferred license. Citation identifies a source; it does not itself grant reuse permission.

The inventory below was checked on November 12, 2026. Full upstream license files are retained under [`sources/review/licenses`](https://github.com/kairosiann/robotics-benchmarks-overview/tree/main/sources/review/licenses), with exact URLs and revision references in [`inventory.json`](https://github.com/kairosiann/robotics-benchmarks-overview/blob/main/sources/review/licenses/inventory.json). The static artifact also includes those texts in `third-party-licenses/`. Preserve those full notices when redistributing the corresponding material. A code repository's license should not be assumed to cover every paper, linked video, dataset, or external asset.

## Figures, webpages, and other benchmark evidence

[`media/credits.json`](https://github.com/kairosiann/robotics-benchmarks-overview/blob/main/media/credits.json) records the original URLs of the reproduced local figures and photographs. Task records carry `source`, `setup_source`, `observation_source`, `evaluation_source`, and media URLs, scopes, and labels where available. Paper and website snapshots remain in `sources/` for auditability; remote videos generally remain links to their official host.

This review did not establish a separate reuse license for every copied paper figure, photograph, entire webpage, or task catalog supplied only through a website. This includes the physical ContactBench website, DataCenterBench, RoboDojo, BEHAVIOR website/paper material, the CoRL pages, and other paper-only sources. NIST and author attribution are recorded, but a government-hosted page is not by itself a determination of the rights in every embedded third-party image or attachment. Consult the specific source's terms or obtain permission where needed. No source snapshot was deleted or relicensed during this audit.

## Publication status

Functional checks have passed, but **licensing is not fully cleared**. The concrete unresolved items are RLBench's custom terms, unconfirmed project-wide HARP/RMMBench permissions, and unverified reuse terms for copied figures/webpages. The inventory preserves the evidence needed to resolve those items without silently discarding sources or claiming rights the catalog does not establish.
