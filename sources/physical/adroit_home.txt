Adroit Hand
¶
This environments consists of a
Shadow Dexterous Hand
attached to a free arm. The system can have up to 30 actuated degrees of freedom. There are 4 possible
environments that can be initialized depending on the task to be solved:
AdroitHandDoor-v1
: The hand has to open a door with a latch.
AdroitHandHammer-v1
: The hand has to hammer a nail inside a board.
AdroitHandPen-v1
: The hand has to manipulate a pen until it achieves a desired goal position and rotation.
AdroitHandRelocate-v1
: The hand has to pick up a ball and move it to a target location.
A sparse reward variant of each environment is also provided.
These environments have a reward of 10.0 for achieving the target goal, and -0.1 otherwise.
They can be initialized via:
AdroitHandDoorSparse-v1
AdroitHandHammerSparse-v1
AdroitHandPenSparse-v1
AdroitHandRelocateSparse-v1
