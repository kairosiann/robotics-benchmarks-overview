Multi-Task Learning
#
In the multi-task learning benchmark, we study training on multi-task pretraining datasets.
We do policy learning on the
Human Pretraining Datasets
, which data across 300 tasks, comprising 65 atomic tasks and 235 composite tasks.
For each task, we provide 100 task demonstrations per task, resulting in
482 hours of total data
.
