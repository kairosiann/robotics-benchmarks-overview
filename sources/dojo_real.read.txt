Real Robot Tasks
RoboDojo also provides a real-robot task suite for evaluating manipulation policies beyond simulation. The suite currently includes
18 real-robot task pages
collected on
Piper X
,
Piper
, and
ARX X5
platforms.
Unlike the simulation task catalog, real-robot tasks are not divided into capability dimensions. They are presented as individual real-world demonstrations, with the robot embodiment listed for each task.
Each task page includes a real-world demo video, natural-language instruction, task description, platform label, embodiment, data source, and intended usage. Use these pages to compare real-robot behavior with simulation tasks and to identify demonstrations for training or evaluation.
The current real-robot suite includes 18 tasks, with 6 tasks on each of three embodiments (Piper X, Piper, and ARX X5) and 100 demonstrations per task.
All real-robot tasks use teleoperated demonstrations and are intended for both training and evaluation.
