Cover Blocks
Your browser does not support the video tag.
Real robot demo
Instruction
Cover the three blocks from left to right, then uncover them again in the order of red, green, and blue.
Description
There are three lids and three colored blocks on the table: red, green, and blue. The robot needs to first cover the blocks from left to right with the lids, memorize the correspondence between the lids and the block colors, and then uncover them again in the order of red, green, and blue.
