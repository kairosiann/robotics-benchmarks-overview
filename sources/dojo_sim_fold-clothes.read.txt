Condition
0

20
When the gripper opens, both sleeves are folded inward close to the opposite chest points.
100
Both sleeves are folded inward, the hems are close to the shoulder points in x/y, the hem line is aligned with the shoulder line within the angle threshold, and the robot returns to origin.
