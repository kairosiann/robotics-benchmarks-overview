Play Tic-Tac-Toe
Your browser does not support the video tag.
Simulation demo
Instruction
Play tic-tac-toe as the first player and fill the board with the opponent.
Description
There is a 3-by-3 tic-tac-toe board. The robot plays as the first player, while the opponent follows a random strategy. The robot and the opponent take turns placing their marks until the board is filled.

Score
Condition
0
No player piece reaches a board cell, or the policy robot moves while the opponent robot arm is moving.
10
Any one player piece is placed on a board cell and the gripper is open.
30
Any two player pieces are placed on board cells.
50
Any three player pieces are placed on board cells.
75
Any four player pieces are placed on board cells.
100
All five player pieces are on board cells at the correct height and the gripper is open.
